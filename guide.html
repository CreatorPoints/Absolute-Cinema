Absolute Cinema Guide

Stuck on something? Need immediate help without a second to spare? This is the right place!

1. How Absolute Cinema Works

Absolute Cinema is a Hack Club You Ship We Ship (YSWS) program where teenagers animate original short films, code rooms to present their film, and assemble together on the day of "Hackclub Film Fantasia" for awards!

Step 1: Animate

2D? 3D? Your choice of a short film under 5 minutes!

Step 2: Build Room

Write an HTML/CSS/JS cinema room featuring an embedded video player and your custom theatre theme. We want you to elaborate more on website.

Step 3: Hackclub Film Fantasia!

We go live on Twitch and YouTube to have a Film Festival to give awards to those who secured positions!

2. Guidelines & Submission Rules

  • Age Eligibility: You must be a high school or middle school student (18 or younger).
  • Original Content:
  • Length: Maximum duration of 5 minutes.
  • Tools & Mediums: Any animation software is permitted (Blender, Maya, FlipaClip, Procreate, Krita, OpenToonz, After Effects, etc.).
  • Code of Conduct: Submissions must follow the Hack Club Code of Conduct. Keep content PG-13, respectful, and safe for everyone.
  • Audio: Ensure all music, sound effects, and voice clips are either original or properly licensed / royalty-free.
💡 Pro Tip: Storyboarding your concept early saves dozens of animation hours. Start with a simple 1-minute pilot before expanding!

3. Building Your Screening Room

Unlike a traditional video contest, your animation lives in its own interactive web environment. You build a custom HTML page that serves as your screening auditorium.

Your room page should include:

  1. An embedded player (e.g. YouTube, Vimeo, or direct HTML5 <video>).
  2. Movie info: Title, synopsis, director/animator credits, and runtime.
  3. Custom styling: Add your own aesthetic, custom seats, themed background, or interactive easter eggs!

To submit your room, fork the Absolute Cinema repository on GitHub, add your room file under rooms/, and open a Pull Request:

git checkout -b room/your-name
# Create rooms/your-name.html
git add rooms/your-name.html
git commit -m "Add screening room for [Film Title]"
git push origin room/your-name
