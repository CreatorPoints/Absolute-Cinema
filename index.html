Guide

A cool YSWS where you make animations and earn cool animation related stuffs to enhance your skill further! Starts Soon... (We don't have sponsors yet)

Sign up and start cooking! 🔥

1. Make a short film

Create any type of short film, 2D or 3d, anytype! Blender or Maya? your choice! Maximum timelimit of 5 minutes!

2. Build your Cinema room to showcase your film on premiere

Yes, you have to make your own cinema room, with iframe to show the film, and submit a PR in the GitHUub.

3. We go LIVE! you get prize according the position secured

We hold a certain date(s) as premiere for every films, and I go live so we all can see how well others cooked, and to fill the seats of the cinema hall!
